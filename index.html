About Our President and Team

Mr. Rudrakumar is the Founder and President of the Kumar Charitable Foundation. He is an engineer by profession and firmly believes that education is the most powerful tool to transform lives and build a better society.

Driven by his passion for helping deserving students, he established the foundation in 2023 with the vision of ensuring that no talented student is forced to discontinue their education because of financial hardship. What began as a small initiative supporting six students has steadily grown into a foundation that now supports more than twenty-five students in pursuing their higher education.

Mr. Rudrakumar personally contributes towards the educational expenses of deserving students and remains actively involved in the foundation's activities. He believes that every student with determination, talent, and strong values deserves an equal opportunity to succeed, regardless of their financial background.

Beyond financial assistance, he motivates students to dream big, work hard, and become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society. His leadership is guided by the principles of integrity, compassion, equality, and service, inspiring both students and volunteers to work together towards creating a brighter future.

Through his dedication and vision, the Kumar Charitable Foundation continues to grow each year, empowering young minds and helping them achieve their academic and career aspirations.

Step-by-Step Process

Scholarship Workflow

Our scholarship selection process is transparent, merit-based, and designed to ensure that financial assistance reaches deserving students.

1

Student Identification

Mrs. Manasa B. V., a teacher and coordinator, identifies academically deserving students based on:

  • Merit
  • Ambition
  • Financial Need
2

Scholarship Application

Students visit the Kumar Charitable Foundation office at Padmanabhanagar and collect the official scholarship application form.

3

Application Submission

Students complete the application form with detailed information explaining:

  • Academic Merit
  • Career Ambition
  • Financial Need

They must attach all required supporting documents such as:

  • SSLC/PU marks cards
  • Income certificate
  • Aadhaar copy
  • College admission details (if available)
  • Any other required documents
4

Personal Interview

Mr. Rudrakumar, along with the student leaders of the foundation, conducts a personal interview to evaluate each applicant.

Selection is based on:

  • Academic Performance
  • Goals and Ambition
  • Financial Background
  • Communication
  • Commitment towards education
5

Scholarship Selection

Applicants who successfully clear the interview are officially selected as scholarship beneficiaries of the Kumar Charitable Foundation.

6

Cheque Distribution

Selected students receive their scholarship cheque from Mr. Basavaraju, Coordinator of the Foundation.

The cheque distribution is conducted in a transparent and organized manner.

7

College Admission

Students use the scholarship amount to secure admission in a recognized college and continue their higher education.

8

Fee Receipt Submission

After completing admission, students submit the official college fee payment receipt to the Foundation for record keeping and scholarship verification.

9

Student Becomes a Foundation Scholar

The student officially becomes a Kumar Charitable Foundation Scholar and continues receiving guidance, motivation, and support throughout the academic journey.
